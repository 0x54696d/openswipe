# OpenSwipe
 
A free, open source photo library cleaner for iOS and Android. Swipe right to keep, swipe left to delete — no subscriptions, no paywalls, everything runs locally on your device.
 
## Features
 
- Swipe-based photo review
- Batch deletion with confirmation before anything is permanently removed
- Support for reviewing videos as well as photos
- Album filtering — clean a specific album or your whole library
- Dark and light theme support

## Privacy
 
Photos and videos never leave your device. OpenSwipe does not collect analytics, crash reports, or any other telemetry. There are no third-party SDKs included. The only data the app stores locally is your session state (which photos you have already reviewed) so you can resume where you left off.

- Follow standard Flutter/Dart style conventions (`flutter analyze` should pass with no issues)
